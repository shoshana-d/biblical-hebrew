Lesson 14

Him, her etc after verbs

Object suffixes much more frequent than freestanding object 4571 vs 951 (37891 verbs, so most don't have suffix or object pronoun after) )but too complicated to learn them so learn to recognize object pronouns, if not there, then object is suffix on end of verb

example with object pronoun: Gen 6:22 Noah did so; just as God commanded him, so he did

example with suffix: Gen 7:5 And Noah did just as the Lord commanded him

  • Suffixes (at the end of a word) on verbs: me, you, him, her, us, them (object pronouns)

There are words for the object pronouns me, you, him, her, us and them in Tanakh hebrew:

אוֹתִי or אֹתִי

me

אוֹתְךָ or אֹתְךָ

you (one male)

אוֹתָךְ or אֹתָךְ

you (one female)

אוֹתוֹ or אֹתוֹ

him

אוֹתָהּ or אֹתָהּ

her

אוֹתָנוּ or אֹתָנוּ

us

אֶתְכֶם

you (several people)

אוֹתָם or אֹתָם

them

examples: on end of verb

sometimes a separate word after a verb eg oto
