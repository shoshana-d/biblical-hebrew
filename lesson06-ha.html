Prefixes (at the beginning of a word) 
 
 
 
 
 
 

These are the 6 Hebrew "words" which never occur as separate words but are always attached to the beginning of another word. These "words" occur more than 100,000 times in the Tanakh, so it's really important to learn them.

ha

ha- frequently corresponds to "the" in the English translation, examples, previous Lesson. but sometimes
  • "the" in translation but no ha- in Hebrew
  • ha- in Hebrew but no "the" in translation הָאֱלֹהִיםwords/w018_haelohim

No Hebrew word for "a", noun without prefix or suffix may be translated "a + noun" Example with "a man" and "the man", about Joseph Gen 37:15 [When he reached Shechem] a man came upon him wandering in the fields. The man asked him "What are you looking for?"
